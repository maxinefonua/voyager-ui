About

This site was born out of a personal project I took on to relearn full-cycle development, and to better organize my travel plans. It is built entirely with open-sourced data, in alignment with the following personal belief.

As developers, our greatest contribution to the digital age is to seamlessly and sustainably increase access to information exchange.
The globalization of society via the internet offers developers the unique opportunity to collaborate, build, and enhance tools within the global digital commons. Such work encourages efficiency, sustainability, and accessibility, all of which reinforce positive feedback loops of improved development.

Below are the technologies and frameworks I used to create this site. To learn more about the Voyager project, feel free to visit my linked GitHub repos. To learn more about myself and other work, feel free to visit my personal site.

Maxine Fonua, Voyager developer
